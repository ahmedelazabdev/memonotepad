Best Practices for Frictionless Note Organization

  1. Capture First, Organize Later: Do not let folder structures hinder speed. Type immediately on the scratchpad, and extract action items once ideas are solidified.
  2. Use Timestamps for Context: Insert timestamps when recording logs, meeting updates, or ongoing task progress to preserve chronological context.
  3. Export Vital Records: While local browser storage is robust and resilient to tabs closing, periodically export critical notes to .txt or .md for permanent archiving.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will my notes remain intact if I clear my browser history? +
Clearing standard browsing history and cookies preserves your notes. However, selecting "Clear site data" or "Clear local storage" in browser settings will erase local client data. We recommend exporting important memos to .txt or .md files.
Does this notepad require an internet connection to run? +
No. The editor runs entirely client-side inside your browser engine. You can write, organize, auto-save, and export notes completely offline.
Is there a limit on how many memos I can store? +
Browser localStorage provides approximately 5MB to 10MB of storage dedicated to this domain, which accommodates tens of thousands of text pages and memos without performance degradation.
